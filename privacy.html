모으는 정보

이 앱은 개인정보를 모으지 않습니다. 회원가입이 없고, 광고 · 분석 · 추적 도구를 쓰지 않으며, 개발자가 사용자의 정보를 받는 서버도 없습니다. 쿠키 같은 자동 수집 장치도 쓰지 않습니다.

기기에 저장되는 것

  • 공부 기록, 설정, 단어장, 직접 입력한 답안, 날짜별 메모, 「이 문장 이상해요」로 남긴 신고, 알림 설정은 이 기기 안에만 저장됩니다. 개발자를 포함해 누구에게도 전송되지 않습니다.
  • 종이에 직접 쓴 문장을 카메라로 찍어 남긴 사진도 이 기기에만 저장되고, 밖으로 보내지 않습니다.
  • iCloud 백업이나 컴퓨터 백업을 켜 두셨다면, iOS 가 이 앱의 기록과 사진도 그 백업에 함께 담습니다. 이것은 iOS 의 백업 기능이며 앱이 보내는 것이 아닙니다. 개발자는 그 백업을 볼 수 없습니다. 앱을 지운 뒤에도 그 전에 만든 백업에는 남아 있을 수 있습니다.
  • 앱을 지우면 이 자료도 함께 지워집니다. 앱을 지우지 않고 모두 지우려면 설정 → 「기록 꺼내기 · 초기화」 → 「모든 언어 · 설정까지 지우기」를 누르세요(받아 둔 추가 자료 파일만 남습니다). 설정의 「기록 꺼내기 · 초기화」 → 「파일로 꺼내기」로 직접 내보낸 파일은 사용자가 고른 곳에 남습니다.

이메일로 문의 · 신고하실 때

  • 보내신 이메일 주소, 적으신 이름, 내용, 첨부 파일(예: 「이 문장 이상해요」 기록)을 받습니다. 이것은 앱이 아니라 사용자가 직접 보내는 것입니다.
  • 쓰는 곳: 답장과 앱 내용 · 오류 고치기에만 씁니다. 광고나 다른 목적으로 쓰지 않고, 다른 사람에게 주지 않습니다(법령에 따른 요구가 있을 때는 예외).
  • 보관: 처리를 마친 날부터 1년 안에 메일함과 휴지통에서 지워 되살릴 수 없게 합니다. 신고 내용 가운데 문장 고치기에 쓴 부분은 누구인지 알 수 없게 한 뒤 남길 수 있습니다.
  • 국외 이전(보관): 문의 메일은 Gmail 로 주고받으므로 국외(나라 밖)에 보관될 수 있습니다. 앱 이용에 딸린 문의 답장과 오류 고치기에 필요한 보관이어서, 이 처리방침에 공개하는 것으로 알립니다.

Privacy Policy

Harupio · Effective 2026-11-17

What we collect

This app does not collect personal information. There is no account and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and the developer has no server that receives your information. The app also uses no cookies or other automatic data collection tools.

What stays on your device

  • Your study records, settings, word lists, typed answers, daily memos, "Something’s wrong here" reports and notification settings are stored only on this device. They are not sent to anyone, including the developer.
  • Photos you take of sentences you wrote on paper also stay only on this device and are never sent anywhere.
  • If you have turned on iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS includes this app's records and photos in that backup. This is part of iOS backup; the app itself does not send them. The developer cannot see your backups. Backups made before you delete the app may still contain them.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. To erase everything without deleting the app, go to Settings → "Export and reset" → "Erase all languages and settings" (only downloaded additional data files remain). A file you export yourself (Settings → "Export and reset" → "Export to a file") stays wherever you saved it.

If you email us

  • We receive your email address, any name you give, your message and any attachment (for example, your "Something’s wrong here" reports). You send these yourself; the app does not.
  • Use: only to reply to you and to fix the app's content or bugs. We do not use them for advertising or any other purpose, and we do not share them with anyone, except where the law requires it. Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in answering you and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: within one year after your request is handled, the email is deleted from the mailbox and trash so that it cannot be recovered. Parts of a report used to fix a sentence may be kept after removing anything that identifies you.
  • International transfer (storage): email is handled through Gmail, so it is stored on Google's servers, which may be in a country other than yours. We disclose this here because this storage is needed to reply to your questions about the app and to fix problems in it.
    • What: your email address, name, message and attachments
    • Where, when and how: in the United States or other countries where Google has servers; when the email is sent and received; over the network
    • Recipient: Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA); contact: see the contact options in Google's Privacy Policy
    • Recipient's purpose and retention: storing the email, for the retention period above. Google protects this information under its own privacy policy.
    • How to refuse and what happens: if you do not email us, nothing is transferred. We then cannot receive your questions or reports by email, but the app works exactly the same.
  • Security: email is handled only in a dedicated support account protected by two-step verification. The app never sends your study records off your device, so the developer keeps none of them. iCloud backups you turn on are handled by Apple.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the email information you sent, or to stop using it, by writing to the contact below; we will act without undue delay. A parent or guardian of a child under 13 (under 14 in Korea) can make the same requests. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Study records on your device can be deleted at any time in Settings → "Export and reset" → "Erase all languages and settings", or by deleting the app.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you save a photo of a sentence you wrote on paper. The photo stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Saving to Photos: only when you save a growth card image. The app never reads your photo library.
  • Notifications: local study reminders, only if you turn them on.

Additional data

To respect their license terms, kanji stroke order (KanjiVG), English level words (based on wordfreq) and Chinese stroke order (Make Me a Hanzi) are not bundled with the app. When you tap "Get additional data" in Settings, these data files are downloaded from the public web address where they are hosted and stored on your device. The app sends no information, but that server (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.) may keep an access log (such as your IP address), as with any web visit. Everything else works without this data.

New sentence packs

New sentences made after the app's release are delivered in packs. Only when you tap "Check for new sentences" or "Download" in Settings does the app connect to the public web address where the packs are hosted, download the list and the sentence files (data only, with the developer's signature checked), and store them on your device. The app sends no information, but that server (also on GitHub Pages) may keep an access log (such as your IP address), as with any web visit.

Children

The app collects no one's information, including children's. Children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) should email us only with a parent or guardian. If we learn that we received a child's information without parental consent, we delete it right away.

For kids: This app never asks for your name or how to reach you. It never sends your study records anywhere. If you want to email us, always do it with a grown-up.
